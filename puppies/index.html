How reserving works

Four steps, about three weeks start to finish.

  1. Text or email us

    Tell us about your house, your yard, your kids, your other animals, and your work hours. We answer every message, usually same day.

  2. Video call with the litter

    Twenty minutes, live, so you can see them move around and ask everything. We'll tell you honestly which pup we think fits you, even if it isn't the one you picked from the photos.

  3. $500 deposit holds your pup

    Applied to the price, refundable up until the go-home date if anything changes on your end. We send a written agreement to sign first.

  4. Pickup day, October 31

    Come to the house between 9am and 4pm. Balance due at pickup, cash or Zelle. Plan on an hour — we go through the folder with you and send you off with food they already eat.
