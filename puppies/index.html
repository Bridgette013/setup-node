Before you text

Read this part.

Huskies are the most returned breed at Arizona shelters. We would rather talk you out of one now than take one back in a year. If all four of these sound fine to you, you're our people.

Arizona specific

From May to September, walks happen before sunrise or after dark. Pavement burns paws at 8am. They will be indoor dogs on A/C for a third of the year, and they'll still blow their coat all over your house.

  • They are loud Not barking — howling, screaming, arguing with you. Thin apartment walls will not survive it.
  • They escape Six-foot fence, dug-proof at the base, latched gates. They climb, they dig, they bolt through open doors.
  • They don't come back This is not an off-leash breed. Recall is unreliable no matter how well you train.
  • They shed twice, hugely Coat blows out in clumps spring and fall. Plan on a vacuum you don't love.
  • They need a job A bored husky redecorates. Two solid outlets a day, minimum, for the next 12–14 years.
