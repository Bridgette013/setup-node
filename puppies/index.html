The breed

What a husky is like.

Siberians are athletic, affectionate, and endlessly social. They were bred to work alongside people all day and then sleep in the tent with them at night. Give one a real outlet and you get one of the most rewarding dogs there is.

Arizona raised

These four were born here and know nothing else. Early-morning walks, shade, and indoor A/C in the summer — that double coat insulates against heat as well as cold. Plenty of huskies live happy lives in the valley.

  • Talkative Huskies hold conversations — howls, grumbles, opinions. It's the best part of owning one.
  • Athletic Built to move. A daily run, hike, or backyard sprint and you'll have a settled dog indoors.
  • People dogs Raised underfoot in a busy house. Good with kids, good with company, happiest in the middle of everything.
  • Smart and curious They learn fast and stay interested. A secure yard and a job to do is all they ask.
  • Striking Thick coats, blue and bi-colored eyes, and the kind of face that stops people on a walk.
